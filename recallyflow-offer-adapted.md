# Offer Brief

## Brand Name

**Recally Flow**

---

## Core Offer

Recally Flow provides a missed revenue recovery system for appointment-based healthcare and beauty practices.

Instead of helping practices only reactivate inactive patients or clients, Recally Flow helps them recover revenue across the full appointment lifecycle:

1. **Recover** inactive patients and clients who have not returned.
2. **Retain** existing patients and clients through loyalty, attendance rewards, and smarter follow-up.
3. **Refill** empty appointment slots caused by cancellations, late changes, and no-shows.

In practical terms, Recally Flow helps clinics, med spas, salons, and other appointment-based practices generate more booked appointments, reduce wasted schedule capacity, increase repeat visits, and recover revenue from the patient or client base they already have.

The system is designed to increase revenue without adding more chaos, more manual follow-up, or more work for the front desk.

## Core Problems We Solve

Appointment-based practices lose revenue because the customer journey is full of leaks.

### Inactive patients and clients

People visit once, finish a treatment, miss a follow-up, or become overdue for a recall, and then never come back because there is no consistent reactivation system.

### No-shows

Clients book appointments but do not show up. The practice loses the revenue from that time slot, while staff time and schedule capacity are wasted.

### Late cancellations

Clients cancel too late for the business to manually refill the slot, causing the schedule to lose revenue even when there may be other clients willing to book.

### Empty last-minute slots

Openings appear in the schedule, but the business has no fast system for promoting those slots to the right people with the right incentive.

### Weak repeat visit behavior

Clients do not always understand the value of regular visits, maintenance, follow-ups, or ongoing care. Without a structured loyalty or perk system, there is less incentive to return consistently.

### Fragmented data

Many practices have clients spread across booking tools, spreadsheets, old systems, paper records, Instagram DMs, or poorly maintained databases. This makes follow-up inconsistent and hard to scale.

### Manual staff workload

Front desk staff are expected to chase follow-ups, confirm appointments, reactivate clients, call no-shows, and fill gaps manually. This creates inconsistent execution and overloads the team.

## How We Solve It

Recally Flow solves these problems with an automated revenue recovery process built around Recover, Retain, and Refill.

### Recover: Reactivation & Recall Automation

The system identifies inactive, overdue, or missed-follow-up patients and clients, then triggers personalized SMS, email, or staff-assisted follow-up campaigns.

Examples:

- patients overdue for hygiene recall or checkups
- med spa clients due for treatment maintenance
- salon clients who have not booked in 8–12 weeks
- clients who completed a service but never booked the next step
- patients who missed a post-treatment follow-up

The goal is to turn dormant records into booked appointments.

### Retain: Loyalty & Attendance Incentives

The system encourages repeat visits and better appointment behavior through loyalty-based flows.

Instead of punishing clients for bad behavior, the practice rewards clients who:

- show up on time
- cancel or reschedule early enough
- book their next visit consistently
- complete 3, 6, or 9 visits
- maintain their treatment or care schedule
- stay active over time

This can be positioned as a **Client Perks**, **Priority Patient**, or **Regular Client Rewards** system.

The message is:

**We value clients who respect their appointments, and we reward them for staying consistent.**

This reduces no-shows without making the practice sound harsh or transactional.

### Refill: Last-Minute Booking Rescue

When a slot opens because of a cancellation, reschedule, or unconfirmed appointment, the system promotes that slot to a relevant segment of patients or clients.

Examples:

- “A priority slot opened today at 4:30 PM.”
- “We have one last-minute appointment available tomorrow.”
- “A cancellation opened a spot for existing clients this afternoon.”
- “Book this available slot today and receive a special client perk.”

The logic is simple:

**An empty slot generates zero revenue. A refilled slot recovers revenue that would otherwise be lost.**

The system can prioritize different segments depending on the business:

- inactive clients
- overdue clients
- VIP clients
- nearby clients
- clients who requested earlier appointments
- clients interested in the same service
- clients who usually book similar time windows

## Primary Use Case

The primary wedge remains **inactive patient or client reactivation**, because it is the easiest to explain and the fastest to validate.

The system identifies people who have not visited in a defined period, such as 3, 6, 9, or 12 months, and moves them into a personalized follow-up flow.

A typical flow may include:

1. SMS reminder or reactivation message
2. Email follow-up with a clear reason to return
3. Optional loyalty or return incentive
4. Staff task if the client does not respond
5. Exit from the flow once the client books
6. Performance tracking based on booked appointments and recovered revenue

This remains the strongest entry point because it uses the practice’s existing data and connects directly to revenue recovery.

## Suggested Loyalty Concept

The loyalty system should be framed as a behavior-shaping client experience, not as a discount program.

Possible names:

- Priority Client Perks
- Regular Client Rewards
- Patient Loyalty Perks
- Consistency Rewards
- Priority Booking Club
- VIP Return Program
- Appointment Respect Rewards

Core idea:

Clients earn or keep perks when they show up, book consistently, or cancel early enough for the practice to refill the slot.

Example rule:

**After 3, 6, and 9 completed visits, clients unlock specific perks. As long as they attend their appointments or cancel in advance, they keep their status and benefits. Direct no-shows may pause or reset certain perks.**

This creates a positive incentive:

**We respect your time, and we reward clients who respect their appointments.**

Suggested perks:

- priority booking access
- early access to cancellation slots
- small service upgrades
- birthday perks
- maintenance reminders
- exclusive client-only offers
- free consultation or check-in
- treatment add-on
- family or friend perk
- limited last-minute booking benefit

Important note:

The loyalty system should avoid relying too heavily on discounts. Status, priority, convenience, small upgrades, and personalized care often feel more premium and protect margins better than constant price cuts.

## Suggested Last-Minute Booking Concept

The last-minute booking system should be framed as **slot rescue**, not desperation discounting.

Possible names:

- Last-Minute Slot Rescue
- Priority Slot Alerts
- Open Slot Recovery
- Cancellation Slot Alerts
- Same-Day Booking Recovery
- Empty Slot Rescue

Core idea:

When an appointment opens, the system sends a targeted message to the most relevant segment of clients.

Example messages:

**A priority slot just opened tomorrow at 3:00 PM. Reply YES to reserve it.**

**We had a cancellation today at 5:30 PM. Existing clients can claim this slot with a special same-day perk.**

**One appointment opened this week for [service]. Would you like us to hold it for you?**

The value proposition:

**Every empty slot is lost revenue. Recally Flow helps refill those slots before they disappear.**
